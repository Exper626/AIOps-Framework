Designed as a tri-band device that leverages the 6 GHz spectrum, the AP37 increases capacity and spectrum efficiency to support more users and devices with consistent, high-quality experiences. With integrated virtual BLE (vBLE), it enables accurate, scalable location services without battery beacons or manual calibration.
