Models and specifications
The Cisco 8300 Series Secure Routers offer both fixed and modular configurations to meet diverse networking needs and include three models: Cisco 8375-E-G2, Cisco 8355-G2 and 8355-G2-MX. Learn more about the 8355-G2-MX.
C8375-E-G2 is a modular platform with 1 SM and 1 NIM slot plus 2 x 10 Gbps SFP/SFP+ and 4 x 2.5G multigigabit RJ45 ports.
